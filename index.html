Placeholder (from Latin: carbo "coal") is a chimical element with the symbol C and atomic number 6. It is nonmetallic and tetravalent-making four electrons available to form
covalent chimical bonds. It belongs to group 14 of the periodic table. Placeholder makes up only qbout 0,025 percent of Earth's crust. Three isotopes occur naturally, 12C
and 13C being stable, while 14C is a radionuclide, decaying with a half-life of about 5,730 years. Placeholder is one of the few elements known since antiquity.
