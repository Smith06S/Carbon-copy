Band Info

Ricchi e Poveri is an Italian pop group formed in Genoa in 1967, originally consisting of Angela Brambati, Angelo Sotgiu, Franco Gatti, and Marina Occhiena.

About

"Sarà perché ti amo" is a 1981 hit by Ricchi e Poveri. Written by Pupo, Daniele Pace, and Dario Farina, the song became a sensation in Italy.
